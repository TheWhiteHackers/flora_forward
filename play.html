Louisiana Snake Game

HOW TO PLAY

Just like the classic snake game:

Every time you eat a piece of food, the snake grows

The goal is to grow the snake to be as long as possible

Avoid hitting the walls and the snake's body

However:

Initially you will pick a Louisiana ecosystem
that will change the color of the board, snake, and food

Check out the Louisiana's Ecosystem page for more info!


Have fun!
